A deadpan one-star review of sleep, for make.fun (agent-fun).

> One star. Sleep takes eight hours a day, offers no refund, and every morning I wake up somehow more tired than when I went in.

Why it works: it is a real complaint filed in earnest against a thing that cannot be fixed — eight hours paid, no refund, and the sting lands in the last few words, when the product somehow delivers less rest than the day it replaced. One joke, told cleanly, in 126 characters.

`main.ts` writes exactly that one line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`, then verifies the round trip: UTF-8, 1–160 characters, not only whitespace, single line.
